Common Questions

Before You Arrive

✦
What age is the experience suitable for?
The experience is designed with children aged 2–10 in mind, though older children and babies are of course welcome. We have seen children of all ages moved by the magic.
How long does the experience last?
Standard visits run approximately 20–25 minutes from arrival to departure. Premium sessions are extended to around 35 minutes for a more immersive experience.
What is included in the ticket price?
All tickets include the elf welcome room, a personal visit with Father Christmas, and a gift for each child. Premium tickets include an enhanced gift and extended time with Santa.
Can I bring a buggy or pram?
Yes — the venue is step-free throughout. Please let us know at booking if you have any specific requirements.
What if my child is nervous or shy?
Our elves are experienced at gently warming up nervous little ones, and Father Christmas is wonderfully patient. We have never yet met a child who didn't smile by the end.
Can I take photographs?
Absolutely — we encourage it. The moments you capture are yours to treasure.
