Your Evening with Father Christmas

What to Expect

✦
I
Arrive at Your Appointed Time

No queuing in the cold. Your timed entry means the door opens precisely when it should — a calm, warm welcome from the moment you arrive.

II
The Elf Workshop

Step into our festive waiting room, looked after by merry elves who fill the air with Christmas magic, stories, and excitement before the big moment.

III
Meeting Father Christmas

Each family is called forward on their own — no crowds, no rush. A truly personal three to four minutes with Santa himself, who has been looking forward to meeting your little ones.

IV
A Gift to Remember

Santa places a thoughtfully chosen, genuinely lovely gift into every child's hands. Not a token — a real present, chosen with care, to make the moment last long after Christmas morning.
